Why Expatagogo

We stopped waiting for “someday.”

After years of working, saving, downsizing, and planning, we chose a different kind of retirement: traveling slowly, living internationally, and learning what freedom really costs.

Expatagogo is where we will share what works, what does not, and what we wish we had known before taking the leap.
